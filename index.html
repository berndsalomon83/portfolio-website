01 About

Out to the edge of what's possible, and back with something usable.

Hej! I'm Bernd, a senior developer and solution architect. For more than twenty years I built and ran large data platforms and business applications inside the Deutsche Bahn group, owning everything from requirements and architecture decisions to implementation and day-to-day production.

Moving to Sweden was a big step: 1,500 km from my old home, a new language to learn, a new culture to grow into. But when I'm in, no step is too big.

I work spec-driven, with security by design, tests first and decisions written down. I bring the same discipline to AI: I steer it deliberately instead of letting it run blind.

Honestly? I love this work. The problem nobody has cracked yet, the moment a design finally clicks, and the last stretch that turns something clever into something people enjoy using: that's what I live for.
