The short version

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it stores nothing on your device. Fonts and scripts are served by this website itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other third party. Personal data is only processed when the hosting provider delivers the pages to you, and when you get in touch with me.

If you write to me or call me, I process what you share (such as your name, your contact details and your message) to answer you and, if we decide to work together, to prepare and carry out our contract. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for enquiries about working together, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering messages).

My e-mail is provided by Google (Gmail), Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Messages may also be processed by Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

I delete your messages once they are no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep them, for example as accounting records.

The “Say hej” button only opens your own e-mail program and copies my address to your clipboard. Nothing is sent until you send the e-mail yourself.

4. Links to other websites

The GitHub and LinkedIn buttons are plain links. No data goes to these services unless you click them; from then on, their privacy policies apply.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16), deleted (Art. 17) or restricted (Art. 18), and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20). You can object at any time to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). An e-mail to the address above is all it takes.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), Box 8114, 104 20 Stockholm, imy.se. You can also turn to the authority where you live or work.

I don't use automated decision-making or profiling.
